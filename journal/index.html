Suspense account

What to do when the two totals of the trial balance refuse to agree.

The one-line rule

One side missing or wrong → suspense

A suspense account opens when
  • only one side of an entry was posted
  • both entries went on the same side
  • the two sides were posted at different amounts
  • an account was overcast or undercast
How to close it
  1. Put the trial balance difference in as the opening balance.
  2. Journalise each error; any one-sided error has suspense as its other half.
  3. Post every suspense line into the account.
  4. If your work is right, it closes to nil.

Tips to rememberThe difference goes in first, on whichever side makes the trial balance agree. Every one-sided error you correct pushes an amount into suspense on the opposite side. When the last error is cleared the account must total to nil — if it does not, one of your corrections is wrong.
